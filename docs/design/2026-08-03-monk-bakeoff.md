# Monk third-caster bake-off: three level-3 bundles

**Status:** owner comparison packet; ready for design review, not approved for
seeding or playtest. The working names, curated lists, and original rules text
remain provisional under D169. This packet makes no recommendation.

## Source boundary and fixed chassis

This is a clean-room design using only the six subclass guidelines, the monk
third-caster pitch catalog, SRD 5.2.1, and D169, D174, D189, and D190 in the
decisions record. The three candidates share these constraints:

- Monk subclass features arrive at Monk levels 3, 6, 11, and 17. The level-3
  spellcasting grant plus one engine feature is a deliberate 3×2 bundle.
- Wisdom is the spellcasting ability. The fixed lists reach about ten prepared
  spells, following the corpus's spell-grant convergence, and add spells only
  when the third-caster first gains slots of their level.
- Focus Points are the only engine currency. A Focus spend buys an effect,
  rider, or reliability; it never raises a spell's slot level.
- No candidate adds concentration-scaled strike damage or replaces an attack
  of the Attack action with a cantrip. No working name uses the "Way of the
  Arcane ___" pattern.
- Monk dependence is Medium. These engines add control, reliability, or
  utility to existing actions; none attempts to repair the base class's damage
  or defense.

### Shared level-6 feature

#### Level 6: Spellwoven Flurry

While you aren't holding a weapon, whenever you use Flurry of Blows, you can
replace one of the Unarmed Strikes it grants with casting a cantrip you know
that has a casting time of an action. The cantrip is cast as part of the Bonus
Action you take to use Flurry of Blows, and you can't replace more than one
Unarmed Strike in this way each time you use that Bonus Action.

This is D189's deliberate exception to the catalog's distance rule: the cantrip
replaces a strike rather than adding to the Flurry, rides the Focus-funded Bonus
Action rather than the Attack action, and requires the monk to be holding no
weapon.

## Candidate 1: bard-list merge

### Warrior of the Barbed Court

**Working name — owner approval pending under D169.**

**Fantasy.** A monk who turns an insult into a supernatural pressure-point and
goad in order to make a foe falter whenever it lashes out at anyone else.

**Three scene moments.**

- **Combat:** the monk lands a cutting spell, names the foe's weakness, and
  forces it to choose between turning on the monk or making a compromised
  attack against an ally.
- **Exploration:** the monk sends a needling whisper past a barrier, drawing a
  watcher into revealing itself or leaving its post.
- **Social:** the monk punctures a bully's performance with a perfectly timed
  command or laugh, changing who controls the room without drawing a weapon.

### Curated spells

**OWNER APPROVAL REQUIRED — CURATED LIST.**

| Monk level | Spell level | Always-prepared additions |
|---:|---:|---|
| 3 | 1 | Bane, Command, Dissonant Whispers, Hideous Laughter |
| 7 | 2 | Enthrall, Suggestion |
| 13 | 3 | Hypnotic Pattern, Tongues |
| 19 | 4 | Compulsion, Confusion |

**Cantrips known:** at Monk level 3, Vicious Mockery and Prestidigitation; at
Monk level 10, Message.

The ten-spell list combines the pressure-point insult, the goad, public control,
and enough social/exploration presence to keep the identity from becoming only
a combat penalty.

### Level-3 bundle: full rules text

#### Level 3: Barbed Court Spellcasting

You have learned to lace disciplined words with magic.

**Cantrips.** You know the cantrips shown on the Barbed Court curated list.

**Prepared Spells.** You always have the spells in the Barbed Court curated
list prepared when you reach the Monk levels shown there. These spells count as
Monk spells for you.

**Spell Slots.** The Monk Third-Caster Spell Slots table shows how many spell
slots you have to cast your level 1+ Monk spells. To cast one of those spells,
you must expend a slot of the spell's level or higher. You regain all expended
spell slots when you finish a Long Rest.

**Spellcasting Ability.** Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for your Monk
spells. Your spell save DC equals 8 plus your Wisdom modifier and Proficiency
Bonus, and your spell attack modifier equals your Wisdom modifier plus your
Proficiency Bonus.

#### Level 3: Barbed Goad

**OWNER APPROVAL REQUIRED — ENGINE FEATURE.**

Once on each of your turns when one or more creatures fail a saving throw
against a spell you cast that has a Verbal component, you can choose one of
those creatures that can hear you. Until the start of your next turn, that
creature is goaded by you. The first time before then that it makes an attack
roll against a target other than you, it must subtract one roll of your Martial
Arts die from the attack roll.

When you goad a creature in this way, you can expend 1 Focus Point to sharpen
the taunt. If you do, the creature must subtract one roll of your Martial Arts
die from every attack roll it makes against a target other than you for the
duration, rather than only the first such attack roll.

**Level-3 loop.** Observe which foe threatens an ally; choose whether an insult
and, on a failed save, a Focus Point are worth committing; act by casting a
Verbal spell; change the foe's next target decision by making attacks against
others less reliable.

### Later-level jobs

- **Level 6 — Spellwoven Flurry:** use the shared text. Vicious Mockery is the
  signature interaction: it replaces one Flurry strike, and a failed save can
  create the goad; it never adds another strike or cantrip.
- **Level 11 job (provisional):** broaden the established taunt into a
  scene-level social and combat verb by letting one foe's public failure carry
  the insult to a second listener, without adding another mark type.
- **Level 17 job (provisional):** culminate in one decisive challenge that
  protects the group from a chosen foe for a bounded scene while keeping the
  same goad-and-Focus decision center.

### Mini budget and failure pass

| Budget field | Record |
|---|---|
| Schedule / tier | Monk 3, entry tier; rock, not ribbon. |
| At-will / nova | At-will first-attack reduction only after a failed Verbal-spell save; nova is the Focus-funded all-attacks expression for the same short duration. Neither adds damage. |
| Actions / concentration | No new action or concentration. The carrier spell supplies its own action, components, slot, and concentration costs. |
| Cadence / expected relevance | Trigger capped once on each of the monk's turns; expected to be offered in roughly 1–3 combat rounds when the monk casts save spells and useful when a foe threatens someone else. |
| Scaling | Martial Arts die and the native Focus pool scale with Monk level; target count remains one. |
| Host interaction | Uses Wisdom, the Martial Arts die, Focus Points, and spellcasting; at level 6 it can ride the already-funded Flurry. |
| Multiclass export | Low-to-moderate: the spell grant is useful, but the stronger loop requires Monk Focus and its d20 rider scales with the Monk's Martial Arts die. |
| Pillars | Engine: combat. Curated spell grant: combat, exploration, and social. |
| Same-slot comparator | Warrior of the Open Hand's Open Hand Technique can apply control on Flurry hits. Barbed Goad trades that direct multi-hit control for one failed-save setup, one target, ally protection, and an optional additional Focus spend. |

**Top failure-taxonomy risks.**

1. **F8 — Stacking Blindness:** Vicious Mockery's Disadvantage and the Martial
   Arts-die subtraction can affect the same attack. **Mitigation:** the overlap
   protects only a target other than the monk, requires the spell's failed save,
   and affects only the first attack unless a second Focus Point is spent;
   acquisition and one-fight-day playtests must measure the combined miss rate.
2. **F13 — The Bookkeeping Tax:** the goaded creature and short duration must be
   remembered. **Mitigation:** only one creature can be chosen per turn, every
   instance has the same start-of-next-turn expiry, and instances never stack.

## Candidate 2: illusion merge, non-positional

### Warrior of the Ten Selves

**Working name — owner approval pending under D169.**

**Fantasy.** A monk who leaves disciplined images in every gesture in order to
let a rehearsed self catch a blow, repair a missed strike, or misdirect an
observer without ever occupying a second position.

**Three scene moments.**

- **Combat:** a duplicate finishes the motion the monk began, turning one miss
  into a second chance, then is fed Focus rather than allowed to vanish.
- **Exploration:** the monk makes a false obstruction or moving figure, causing
  an observer to stop, investigate, or choose the wrong route.
- **Social:** a borrowed appearance and precisely mimicked manner let the monk
  present the face a tense scene expects while a small image supplies the lie's
  missing detail.

### Curated spells

**OWNER APPROVAL REQUIRED — CURATED LIST.**

| Monk level | Spell level | Always-prepared additions |
|---:|---:|---|
| 3 | 1 | Color Spray, Disguise Self, Silent Image |
| 7 | 2 | Invisibility, Mirror Image, Phantasmal Force |
| 13 | 3 | Hypnotic Pattern, Major Image |
| 19 | 4 | Greater Invisibility, Hallucinatory Terrain |

**Cantrips known:** at Monk level 3, Minor Illusion. No additional fixed
cantrip is granted later.

Mirror Image is the level-7 centerpiece: its three duplicates last long enough
to serve both their printed defense and the martial engine below. Mislead is a
5th-level spell and is outside third-caster reach, so it is not listed.

### Level-3 bundle: full rules text

#### Level 3: Ten Selves Spellcasting

You have learned to give magical form to your practiced feints.

**Cantrips.** You know the cantrip shown on the Ten Selves curated list.

**Prepared Spells.** You always have the spells in the Ten Selves curated list
prepared when you reach the Monk levels shown there. These spells count as Monk
spells for you.

**Spell Slots.** The Monk Third-Caster Spell Slots table shows how many spell
slots you have to cast your level 1+ Monk spells. To cast one of those spells,
you must expend a slot of the spell's level or higher. You regain all expended
spell slots when you finish a Long Rest.

**Spellcasting Ability.** Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for your Monk
spells. Your spell save DC equals 8 plus your Wisdom modifier and Proficiency
Bonus, and your spell attack modifier equals your Wisdom modifier plus your
Proficiency Bonus.

#### Level 3: Rehearsed Self

**OWNER APPROVAL REQUIRED — ENGINE FEATURE.**

Whenever you cast an Illusion spell, if the spell doesn't cause one or more
illusory duplicates of you to appear, one illusory duplicate of you appears in
your space. The duplicate moves with you, mimics your actions, and lasts until
the end of your next turn. It has no effect except as described in this
feature.

Once on each of your turns when you miss with an Unarmed Strike, you can choose
one illusory duplicate of yourself created by a spell you cast and reroll the
attack roll. You must use the new roll, and then the duplicate is destroyed.

Whenever an illusory duplicate of yourself created by a spell you cast would
be destroyed, you can expend 1 Focus Point to prevent it from being destroyed.

**Level-3 loop.** Observe whether an image remains available; choose whether to
spend it on a missed Unarmed Strike and whether it merits Focus to preserve;
act by casting an Illusion spell and making the monk's normal strike; change the
exchange by letting the image complete one failed motion.

### Later-level jobs

- **Level 6 — Spellwoven Flurry:** use the shared text. Minor Illusion can
  replace one Flurry strike and create the short-lived duplicate; that duplicate
  may then support a later strike from the same Flurry if one remains.
- **Level 11 job (provisional):** give one maintained image a bounded physical
  interaction with the scene, adding the Painted World exploration verb without
  moving the monk or originating an attack from another space.
- **Level 17 job (provisional):** make the established duplicates endure through
  a decisive exchange and answer a failed strike or incoming hit more reliably,
  without adding a second illusion subsystem.

### Mini budget and failure pass

| Budget field | Record |
|---|---|
| Schedule / tier | Monk 3, entry tier; rock, not ribbon. |
| At-will / nova | At-will access requires casting an Illusion spell and consumes one duplicate for one mandatory reroll; nova spends Focus to preserve the same duplicate. No extra attack or damage is created. |
| Actions / concentration | No new action or concentration. The carrier spell uses its printed cost; Minor Illusion costs an action at level 3, while Silent Image also occupies concentration. |
| Cadence / expected relevance | Reroll capped once on each of the monk's turns; expected to be offered in 1–3 rounds after an illusion is cast and chosen only after a miss. |
| Scaling | Native Focus capacity, level-6 Flurry delivery, and level-7 Mirror Image access deepen the loop; reroll count stays once per turn. |
| Host interaction | Uses Illusion casting, Unarmed Strikes, Flurry of Blows, and Focus. Mirror Image's existing duplicate count is reused rather than copied into a new pool. |
| Multiclass export | Low: the accuracy repair applies only to Unarmed Strikes, and repeat use consumes Monk Focus. A three-level dip has only action-costly or slotted image creation. |
| Pillars | Engine: combat. Curated spell grant: combat, exploration, and social. |
| Same-slot comparator | Open Hand Technique offers a choice of control after Flurry hits. Rehearsed Self instead repairs at most one miss, requires prior image creation, consumes that image, and Focus buys preservation rather than a new hit. |

**Top failure-taxonomy risks.**

1. **F5 — The Advantage Faucet:** a repeatable reroll can become broad accuracy
   amplification. **Mitigation:** it applies only to one Unarmed Strike per turn,
   consumes a spell-created duplicate, requires the new result, and grants no
   additional roll after success.
2. **F13 — The Bookkeeping Tax:** temporary and Mirror Image duplicates could
   become two counters or implied positions. **Mitigation:** the temporary image
   is created only when the spell created none, Mirror Image's printed count is
   reused, every duplicate stays in the monk's space and moves with the monk,
   and no swap, teleport, remote origin, or second position exists.

## Candidate 3: primal bindings

### Warrior of the Hundred Knots

**Working name — owner approval pending under D169.**

**Fantasy.** A monk who draws living bindings through strikes and surrounding
growth in order to slow a quarry and close the routes by which it could escape.

**Three scene moments.**

- **Combat:** the monk marks a quarry, closes the distance, and lands a strike
  that makes flight difficult before spending Focus to stop it outright.
- **Exploration:** the monk reads and follows a trail, then uses growth, mist,
  or silence of movement to bring the group close without losing the quarry.
- **Social:** the monk speaks with a local Beast to learn what crossed its
  territory and turns that testimony into a route the group can follow.

### Curated spells

**OWNER APPROVAL REQUIRED — CURATED LIST.**

| Monk level | Spell level | Always-prepared additions |
|---:|---:|---|
| 3 | 1 | Ensnaring Strike, Entangle, Hunter's Mark, Speak with Animals |
| 7 | 2 | Pass without Trace, Spike Growth |
| 13 | 3 | Plant Growth, Wind Wall |
| 19 | 4 | Dominate Beast, Locate Creature |

**Cantrips known:** at Monk level 3, Druidcraft and Produce Flame; at Monk
level 10, Guidance.

Thorn Whip and Grasping Vine do not appear in SRD 5.2.1 and therefore are not
used.

### Level-3 bundle: full rules text

#### Level 3: Hundred Knots Spellcasting

You have learned to call roots, wind, and the trail itself into your martial
discipline.

**Cantrips.** You know the cantrips shown on the Hundred Knots curated list.

**Prepared Spells.** You always have the spells in the Hundred Knots curated
list prepared when you reach the Monk levels shown there. These spells count as
Monk spells for you.

**Spell Slots.** The Monk Third-Caster Spell Slots table shows how many spell
slots you have to cast your level 1+ Monk spells. To cast one of those spells,
you must expend a slot of the spell's level or higher. You regain all expended
spell slots when you finish a Long Rest.

**Spellcasting Ability.** Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for your Monk
spells. Your spell save DC equals 8 plus your Wisdom modifier and Proficiency
Bonus, and your spell attack modifier equals your Wisdom modifier plus your
Proficiency Bonus.

#### Level 3: Verdant Bindings

**OWNER APPROVAL REQUIRED — ENGINE FEATURE.**

Your Unarmed Strikes count as weapons for the purpose of casting Ensnaring
Strike.

Once on each of your turns when you hit a creature with an Unarmed Strike, you
can reduce its Speed by 10 feet until the start of your next turn if either of
the following is true:

- the creature is the target of a spell on which you are concentrating; or
- the creature occupies Difficult Terrain created by a spell on which you are
  concentrating.

Immediately after you reduce a creature's Speed in this way, you can expend 1
Focus Point to tighten the binding. The creature must succeed on a Strength
saving throw against your Focus Point save DC or have a Speed of 0 until the
start of your next turn. On a successful save, the 10-foot reduction remains.

**Level-3 loop.** Observe whether the quarry or its ground is held by the monk's
concentration; choose which hit receives the once-per-turn binding and whether
to spend Focus; act through the normal Unarmed Strike; change the battlefield
by slowing the quarry or, after a failed save, stopping it.

### Later-level jobs

- **Level 6 — Spellwoven Flurry:** use the shared text. Produce Flame can
  replace one Flurry strike, but Verdant Bindings still requires an Unarmed
  Strike hit; the cantrip never adds or substitutes for that trigger.
- **Level 11 job (provisional):** broaden the binding from one quarry to a
  scene-level route or boundary that the monk can actively close, adding a
  terrain-control verb rather than more strike damage.
- **Level 17 job (provisional):** culminate in one simple, Focus-heavy binding
  that keeps a chosen quarry in the encounter despite high-tier movement while
  preserving a meaningful successful-save result.

### Mini budget and failure pass

| Budget field | Record |
|---|---|
| Schedule / tier | Monk 3, entry tier; rock, not ribbon. |
| At-will / nova | At-will once-per-turn 10-foot reduction requires both concentration setup and a hit; nova spends Focus and allows a Strength save to set Speed to 0. Neither expression adds damage or accuracy. |
| Actions / concentration | No new action. The engine requires an Unarmed Strike and an eligible concentration spell; Hunter's Mark and Ensnaring Strike also contest the monk's Bonus Action. |
| Cadence / expected relevance | Offered at most once per turn and expected in 2–4 rounds while concentration survives and the monk can reach the affected quarry or terrain. |
| Scaling | Focus save DC and native Focus capacity scale; target count and 10-foot baseline remain fixed. |
| Host interaction | Uses Unarmed Strikes, Wisdom-based Focus DC, Focus Points, movement, and concentration spells. It explicitly makes Ensnaring Strike usable with the subclass's unarmed identity. |
| Multiclass export | Low: the engine requires an Unarmed Strike plus the subclass's narrow concentration relationship, and the stronger stop consumes Monk Focus. |
| Pillars | Engine: combat and pursuit. Curated spell grant: combat, exploration, and limited social information. |
| Same-slot comparator | Open Hand Technique can Push or Topple on each Flurry hit after the Flurry's Focus cost. Verdant Bindings has broader Attack-action availability but is once per turn, needs concentration setup, adds no damage, and charges another Focus Point plus a save for Speed 0. |

**Top failure-taxonomy risks.**

1. **F8 — Stacking Blindness:** Hunter's Mark already multiplies damage across
   the monk's many attacks. **Mitigation:** Verdant Bindings adds no damage,
   attack, or accuracy, and concentration prevents Hunter's Mark, Entangle,
   Ensnaring Strike, and Spike Growth from stacking with one another.
2. **F6 — Action-Type Congestion:** Hunter's Mark and Ensnaring Strike compete
   with Martial Arts and Flurry of Blows for the Bonus Action. **Mitigation:**
   the engine adds no action of its own and rides a normal Unarmed Strike;
   playtest must confirm that choosing setup over the immediate Bonus Action
   attack remains a live choice rather than a trap.

## Dispatch report

### Engine features, verbatim

**Barbed Goad.** Once on each of your turns when one or more creatures fail a
saving throw against a spell you cast that has a Verbal component, you can
choose one of those creatures that can hear you. Until the start of your next
turn, that creature is goaded by you. The first time before then that it makes
an attack roll against a target other than you, it must subtract one roll of

the taunt. If you do, the creature must subtract one roll of your Martial Arts

of you appears in your space. The duplicate moves with you, mimics your actions,
and lasts until the end of your next turn. It has no effect except as described

one illusory duplicate of yourself created by a spell you cast and reroll the
attack roll. You must use the new roll, and then the duplicate is destroyed.

Whenever an illusory duplicate of yourself created by a spell you cast would
be destroyed, you can expend 1 Focus Point to prevent it from being destroyed.

**Verdant Bindings.** Your Unarmed Strikes count as weapons for the purpose of
casting Ensnaring Strike.

can reduce its Speed by 10 feet until the start of your next turn if either of
the following is true:

- the creature is the target of a spell on which you are concentrating; or
- the creature occupies Difficult Terrain created by a spell on which you are
  concentrating.

Immediately after you reduce a creature's Speed in this way, you can expend 1
Focus Point to tighten the binding. The creature must succeed on a Strength
saving throw against your Focus Point save DC or have a Speed of 0 until the
start of your next turn. On a successful save, the 10-foot reduction remains.

### Focus levers

- **Barbed Goad:** one Focus Point makes the Martial Arts-die penalty apply to
  every attack against another target during the same short duration instead
  of only the first.
- **Rehearsed Self:** one Focus Point prevents a spell-created duplicate from
  being destroyed, preserving either its printed defense or a later martial
  reroll.
- **Verdant Bindings:** one Focus Point asks for a Strength save to turn the
  existing 10-foot reduction into Speed 0; success keeps the baseline slow.

None changes a spell's slot level.

### Curated spells by entry

- **Barbed Court:** Bane, Command, Dissonant Whispers, Hideous Laughter;
  Enthrall, Suggestion; Hypnotic Pattern, Tongues; Compulsion, Confusion.
  Cantrips: Vicious Mockery, Prestidigitation, Message.
- **Ten Selves:** Color Spray, Disguise Self, Silent Image; Invisibility,
  Mirror Image, Phantasmal Force; Hypnotic Pattern, Major Image; Greater
  Invisibility, Hallucinatory Terrain. Cantrip: Minor Illusion.
- **Hundred Knots:** Ensnaring Strike, Entangle, Hunter's Mark, Speak with
  Animals; Pass without Trace, Spike Growth; Plant Growth, Wind Wall; Dominate
  Beast, Locate Creature. Cantrips: Druidcraft, Produce Flame, Guidance.

### Binding decisions that constrained the draft

- **D169** required an owner-original Monk third-caster, Wisdom slot chassis,
  and owner approval before seeding. Consequently every name and original rule
  remains provisional, and no retired third-caster subclass was used as a text
  source or mechanical template.
- **D174** required mechanical as well as naming distance. The designs therefore
  avoid slot conversion, concentration-scaled strike riders, and an Attack-action
  cantrip replacement even where those would have been straightforward bridges
  between casting and martial turns.
- **D189** overrode the catalog's general attack-replacement warning only for a
  redesigned, weapon-free Flurry. The exact level-6 text replaces one strike,
  costs the Flurry's Focus Point, and never touches the Attack action; without
  that ruling, no cantrip replacement would appear.
- **D190** forced every stronger level-3 expression onto Focus rather than a new
  pool. It also removed the illusion candidate's tempting dual-position,
  remote-origin, swap, and teleport mechanics, made Mirror Image the level-7
  centerpiece, merged mockery and taunt into one mark, and fixed Hunter's Mark,
  Ensnaring Strike, and Entangle as the primal core.
- The verified-source rulings excluded Mislead as a 5th-level spell and excluded
  Thorn Whip and Grasping Vine because they are absent from SRD 5.2.1. Those
  would otherwise have been natural high-level illusion and binding choices.
