# Warrior of the Waking Will

OWNER-APPROVAL: Subclass name, “Warrior of the Waking Will.”

Status: **provisional, ready for design review only**. This is a MINT-FREE,
docs-only full subclass draft. The family chassis is reproduced without
mechanical change; every authored name, list, and feature remains subject to
owner approval and playtest.

## 1. Identity

OWNER-APPROVAL: Identity paragraph and ancestry disclosure.

A Warrior of the Waking Will carries a chosen thought through motion and
impact in order to make the body continue the form the mind has commanded. Its
repeated visible verb is **sustain**: in combat a blow lands but the monk's
blurred form holds, in exploration disciplined motion carries the monk beyond
an ordinary march or through a narrow passage, and in social scenes an inward
focus opens another mind or makes an unknown language intelligible.

The 3.5 SRD Psionic Fist is this subclass's open-content concept ancestor; this adaptation uses none of its wording or subsystem mechanics.

**Cantrips.** You know the cantrips shown on the Waking Will curated list.

**Prepared Spells.** You always have the spells in the Waking Will curated
list prepared when you reach the Monk levels shown there. These spells count as
Monk spells for you.

**Spell Slots.** The Monk Third-Caster Spell Slots table shows how many spell
slots you have to cast your level 1+ Monk spells. To cast one of those spells,
you must expend a slot of the spell's level or higher. You regain all expended
spell slots when you finish a Long Rest.

**Spellcasting Ability.** Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for your Monk
spells. Your spell save DC equals 8 plus your Wisdom modifier and Proficiency
Bonus, and your spell attack modifier equals your Wisdom modifier plus your
Proficiency Bonus.

## 3. Subclass Features

The substantive feature schedule is 3, 6, 11, and 17, matching the SRD Monk
and Warrior of the Open Hand schedule ([SRD Monk table](../srd/full/srd-5.2.1.txt#L3022)
and [Open Hand](../srd/full/srd-5.2.1.txt#L3130)). Spellcasting is compact
support beside the level-3 engine.

### Level 3: Unbroken Motion

OWNER-APPROVAL: Entire feature, including the qualifying-spell boundary,
failed-save trigger, and 1-Focus automatic success.

When you fail a Constitution saving throw to maintain Concentration on a Monk
spell prepared by your Waking Will Spellcasting feature, you can expend 1
Focus Point to succeed instead if the spell has a range of Self or you are its
only target. This benefit doesn't prevent the spell from ending for any other
reason.

### Level 6: Spellwoven Flurry

OWNER-APPROVAL: Full rules text and Waking Will cantrip interaction note.

While you aren't holding a weapon, whenever you use Flurry of Blows, you can
replace one of the Unarmed Strikes it grants with casting a cantrip you know
that has a casting time of an action. The cantrip is cast as part of the Bonus
Action you take to use Flurry of Blows, and you can't replace more than one
Unarmed Strike in this way each time you use that Bonus Action.

For this subclass, *Mage Hand* is the signature telekinetic interaction. It can
replace one Flurry strike and create or manipulate with the hand as stated in
the spell, but the hand can't attack and therefore doesn't become an Unarmed
Strike or trigger a feature that requires one. *Message* and *Guidance* can
instead provide communication or support. Each cantrip replaces a strike; none
adds another strike or cantrip.

### Level 11: Thought Before Motion

OWNER-APPROVAL: Entire feature, including the prepared-spell boundary,
self-only boundary, and 2-Focus casting-time change.

When you cast a Monk spell prepared by your Waking Will Spellcasting feature,
you can expend 2 Focus Points to change its casting time to a Bonus Action for
this casting if it normally has a casting time of an action and either has a
range of Self or targets only you.

### Level 17: Perfect Continuity

OWNER-APPROVAL: Entire feature, including immunity to damage-based
Concentration loss and the qualifying-spell boundary.

Your Concentration on a Monk spell prepared by your Waking Will Spellcasting
feature can't be broken as a result of taking damage if the spell has a range
of Self or you are its only target. The spell can still end for any other
reason.

## 4. Power-Budget Worksheet

### Host posture and protected dial

Monk subclass dependence is **Medium**. The base class already supplies
damage, defense, mobility, and a Short-Rest Focus engine, so this subclass
spends most of its budget on a narrow spell list and reliable entry into and
maintenance of self-applied forms. It adds no attack, damage die, Armor Class
bonus, save-DC bonus, remote attack origin, or new currency. The protected dial
is sustaining the spell that makes mind visible through body; spell slots,
Concentration, self-only targeting, Focus, casting time, and Bonus Action
competition price that dial.

The list is a major grant: ten always-prepared level 1–4 spells plus three fixed
cantrips, compared with no spell grant on Warrior of the Open Hand. Several
spells provide printed defense or action economy, so the authored features add
neither damage nor an additional action.

### Explicit numerical checks

- At level 3 with Constitution +2, a DC 10 Concentration save succeeds 65
  percent of the time. Unbroken Motion can turn the 35 percent failure case
  into success for 1 Focus Point. It changes no roll unless damage has already
  threatened a qualifying spell.
- At level 6 with a 65 percent hit chance, Dexterity +4, and a d8 Martial Arts
  die, one strike contributes about `0.65 × 8.5 = 5.53` expected damage.
  Replacing it with *Mage Hand*, *Message*, or *Guidance* deals no direct
  damage; its value must come from the spell's printed utility.
- At level 11 with a 65 percent hit chance, Dexterity +5, and a d10 Martial
  Arts die, two Attack-action strikes contribute about
  `2 × 0.65 × 10.5 = 13.65` expected damage. Thought Before Motion can free the
  Action for those attacks, but consumes the Bonus Action, 2 Focus, and a spell
  slot. Compared with an ordinary Action casting plus one Bonus Action Unarmed
  Strike, the setup turn gains at most one ordinary strike before other spell
  effects are counted.
- At level 17, a Monk with Constitution +2 and Proficiency Bonus +6 succeeds on
  a DC 15 Concentration save 70 percent of the time before the level-14 Focus
  reroll. Perfect Continuity removes that damage-based variance for the narrow
  list, but it doesn't preserve Concentration through Incapacitated or permit
  two Concentration spells.

### Action economy and resource stress

- **Ordinary martial turn:** Attack action; Bonus Action Martial Arts or a
  Focus option; Reaction available; no subclass rider without a qualifying
  spell and failed Concentration save.
- **Self-spell turn:** Action casts the spell; Bonus Action remains available
  for one Unarmed Strike or a Focus option unless the spell itself uses it.
- **Spellwoven turn:** Attack action; weapon-free 1-Focus Flurry Bonus Action
  replaces one strike with a cantrip; the cantrip adds no attack.
- **Thought-before-motion turn:** Bonus Action casts one qualifying spell for 2
  Focus; Action can Attack or cast a cantrip, but the SRD one-slot-per-turn rule
  still prevents another slotted spell and the occupied Bonus Action prevents
  Martial Arts or Flurry.
- **One-fight day:** slots and Focus can be concentrated. A level-13 accelerated
  *Haste* costs 2 Focus and one level-3 slot before any Flurry or Stunning Strike
  spending on later turns; no subclass rule adds an attack to *Haste*.
- **No-Short-Rest day:** cantrips and the base Monk remain; slots and Focus
  deplete normally, and the subclass restores neither.
- **Rest-rich day:** only native Focus returns on a Short Rest. Spell slots
  still return only on a Long Rest, and no output restores either input or
  raises a spell's slot level.

## 7. Distance Checklist

The family distance rules and D193 avoid-list were re-run against the final
mechanics.

1. **List distance — Pass.** The fixed list is a short self-transmutation,
   self-defense, telekinetic, and telepathic slice. It doesn't reproduce the
   siblings' mockery/social-control, illusion-duplicate, or primal
   quarry/terrain lists.
2. **Delivery distance — Pass with D189's explicit exception.** Unbroken Motion,
   Thought Before Motion, and Perfect Continuity sustain or accelerate a spell
   applied only to the monk. They don't goad listeners, create or spend
   duplicates, or bind a concentrated quarry. No Focus-to-upcast,
   concentration-scaled melee rider, remote attack origin, Focus-bought spell
   menu, or Attack-action attack replacement exists. Spellwoven Flurry replaces
   one strike only inside the weapon-free, Focus-funded Bonus Action Flurry.
3. **Name distance — Pass.** “Warrior of the Waking Will” follows the SRD Monk
   house style and uses no Arcane/Mystic/Astral or body-part template.
