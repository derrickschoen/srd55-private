# Warrior of the Ten Selves

Status: **provisional, ready for design review only**. This is a MINT-FREE,
docs-only full subclass draft. The frozen bake-off inputs are reproduced
without mechanical change; authored text remains subject to owner approval and
playtest.

## 1. Identity

OWNER-APPROVAL: Identity paragraph and ancestry disclosure.

A Warrior of the Ten Selves leaves disciplined images in every practiced
gesture in order to let a rehearsed self complete a failed motion or sustain a
needed deception. Its repeated visible verb is **rehearse**: in combat a
duplicate finishes the strike the monk missed, in exploration a precise false
image supplies the route or detail an observer expects, and in social scenes a
borrowed appearance and practiced manner let the monk present the self the room
is prepared to believe. Every duplicate shares the monk's space; none is a
second position.

The 3.5 SRD Psionic Fist is used only as open-content, concept-level precedent
for the frame of a monk who develops supernatural casting; no wording or
mechanical form is taken from it.

**Cantrips.** You know the cantrip shown on the Ten Selves curated list.

**Prepared Spells.** You always have the spells in the Ten Selves curated list
prepared when you reach the Monk levels shown there. These spells count as
Monk spells for you.

**Spell Slots.** The Monk Third-Caster Spell Slots table shows how many spell
slots you have to cast your level 1+ Monk spells. To cast one of those spells,
you must expend a slot of the spell's level or higher. You regain all expended
spell slots when you finish a Long Rest.

**Spellcasting Ability.** Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for your Monk
spells. Your spell save DC equals 8 plus your Wisdom modifier and Proficiency
Bonus, and your spell attack modifier equals your Wisdom modifier plus your
Proficiency Bonus.

## 3. Subclass Features

The substantive feature schedule is 3, 6, 11, and 17, matching the SRD Monk
and Warrior of the Open Hand schedule ([SRD Monk table](../srd/full/srd-5.2.1.txt#L3022)
and [Open Hand](../srd/full/srd-5.2.1.txt#L3130)). Spellcasting is compact
support beside the level-3 engine.

### Level 3: Rehearsed Self

FROZEN BAKE-OFF ENGINE: reproduced without mechanical change.

Whenever you cast an Illusion spell, if the spell doesn't cause one or more
illusory duplicates of you to appear, one illusory duplicate of you appears in
your space. The duplicate moves with you, mimics your actions, and lasts until
the end of your next turn. It has no effect except as described in this
feature.

Once on each of your turns when you miss with an Unarmed Strike, you can choose
one illusory duplicate of yourself created by a spell you cast and reroll the
attack roll. You must use the new roll, and then the duplicate is destroyed.

Whenever an illusory duplicate of yourself created by a spell you cast would
be destroyed, you can expend 1 Focus Point to prevent it from being destroyed.

### Level 6: Spellwoven Flurry

OWNER-APPROVAL: Full rules text and Ten Selves cantrip interaction note.

While you aren't holding a weapon, whenever you use Flurry of Blows, you can
replace one of the Unarmed Strikes it grants with casting a cantrip you know
that has a casting time of an action. The cantrip is cast as part of the Bonus
Action you take to use Flurry of Blows, and you can't replace more than one
Unarmed Strike in this way each time you use that Bonus Action.

For this subclass, *Minor Illusion* can replace one Flurry strike and, because
it is an Illusion spell that creates no duplicate of you, create the short-lived
duplicate from Rehearsed Self. If a later strike from that same Flurry misses,
the duplicate can be used on that miss. The cantrip replaces a strike; it never
adds another strike or cantrip.

### Level 11: Embodied Rehearsal

OWNER-APPROVAL: Entire feature, including the Dexterity-or-Wisdom boundary and
the duplicate cost.

Once on each of your turns when you fail a Dexterity or Wisdom ability check,
you can choose one illusory duplicate of yourself created by a spell you cast
and reroll the ability check. You must use the new roll, and then the duplicate
is destroyed.

Rehearsed Self can prevent that duplicate from being destroyed in the same way
it can preserve a duplicate used for an Unarmed Strike. The duplicate remains
in your space: it completes your practiced interaction, perception, or manner;
it doesn't manipulate anything from another space.

### Level 17: Perfected Rehearsal

OWNER-APPROVAL: Entire feature, including failed-reroll preservation and the
defensive Reaction.

When the new roll granted by Rehearsed Self or Embodied Rehearsal also misses
or fails, the duplicate isn't destroyed.

In addition, when an attack roll hits you, you can take a Reaction and choose
one illusory duplicate of yourself created by a spell you cast. The attacker
must reroll the attack roll and use the new roll, and then the duplicate is
destroyed. Rehearsed Self can prevent the duplicate from being destroyed after
it forces this reroll.

## 4. Power-Budget Worksheet

### Host posture and protected dial

Monk subclass dependence is **Medium**. The subclass redirects spell actions,
Unarmed Strikes, Flurry, and Focus toward bounded roll repair; it doesn't add a
damage rider, attack, action, Armor Class bonus, save-DC bonus, second position,
or new currency. The protected dial is choosing when a spell-created image
should finish a failed motion. Image availability, once-per-turn use, mandatory
new rolls, destruction, action costs, and Focus preservation price that dial.

The fixed list is itself substantial: ten always-prepared level 1–4 spells and
one cantrip, compared with no spell grant on Warrior of the Open Hand. Mirror
Image's three printed duplicates are deliberately reused rather than copied
into another counter.

### Explicit numerical checks

- With a 65% base hit chance, rerolling one miss changes the chance that the
  attack ultimately hits from 65% to `0.65 + 0.35 × 0.65 = 87.75%`, an increase
  of 22.75 percentage points for that one attack. At level 3, d6 + Dexterity +3
  averages 6.5 damage on a hit, so the reroll contributes about
  `0.35 × 0.65 × 6.5 = 1.48` expected damage and consumes a duplicate.
- At level 6, replacing a d8 + Dexterity +4 strike sacrifices about
  `0.65 × 8.5 = 5.53` expected damage. The duplicate's later attack reroll is
  worth about `0.35 × 0.65 × 8.5 = 1.93`; using *Minor Illusion* only for
  damage is therefore a losing exchange, while its illusion and preservation
  choices supply the intended value.
- At level 11, a d10 Martial Arts hit with Dexterity +5 averages 10.5, making
  an eligible attack reroll worth about 2.39 expected damage. Embodied Rehearsal
  adds no combat damage unless the check itself changes the scene.
- If an enemy that normally hits 65% deals 30 damage, forcing it to reroll an
  already successful hit prevents the hit 35% of the time, about 10.5 expected
  damage. At level 17, Deflect Attacks instead reduces an average
  `5.5 + 5 + 17 = 27.5` damage. Perfected Rehearsal is therefore chiefly for a
  critical hit, a dangerous hit rider, or unusually large damage, and it spends
  the same Reaction.

### Action economy and resource stress

- **Ordinary martial turn:** Attack action; Bonus Action Martial Arts or Focus;
  Rehearsed Self is eligible only if a prior image remains.
- **Illusion setup turn:** Action casts most listed illusions; Bonus Action is
  still crowded; a short-lived duplicate lasts through the end of next turn.
- **Flurry setup turn:** Attack action; weapon-free 1-Focus Flurry replaces one
  strike with *Minor Illusion*, then a remaining strike can spend the image.
- **Defensive turn at 17:** Perfected Rehearsal uses the Reaction, so Deflect
  Attacks can't be used in the same round before the Reaction returns.
- **One-fight day:** slots, Mirror Image duplicates, and Focus can be
  concentrated, but every roll repair is capped and images are still consumed
  unless Focus is spent.
- **No-Short-Rest / rest-rich days:** at-will *Minor Illusion* plus ordinary
  actions preserves access; native Focus alone recovers on Short Rest, and no
  feature restores its own input.

## 7. Distance Checklist

The three pitch-catalog rules were re-run against the final mechanics.

1. **List distance — Pass.** The fixed list is an Illusion-school slice used
   through rehearsal and misdirection rather than a general arcane list.
2. **Delivery distance — Pass with D189's explicit exception.** No Focus-to-upcast,
   concentration-scaled strike damage, or Focus-bought spell menu exists.
   Spellwoven Flurry replaces one strike only inside the weapon-free,
   Focus-funded Bonus Action Flurry. Every duplicate remains in the monk's
   space: no swap, teleport, remote attack origin, or dual-position rule exists.
3. **Name distance — Pass.** “Warrior of the Ten Selves” follows the SRD Monk
   house style and uses no Arcane/Mystic/Astral or body-part template.
