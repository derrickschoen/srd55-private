## Number checks — independently derived from the SRD extract

Source: `docs/srd/source/draconic-resilience.txt`. It says that Draconic
Resilience increases maximum HP by 3 when gained, adds 1 for each later
Sorcerer level, and—while unarmored—sets base AC to 10 plus Dexterity and
Charisma modifiers.

HP derivation:

- Class subtotal: Sorcerer 1 `(6 + CON 1) = 7`; multiclass Warlock 1
  `(5 + CON 1) = 6`; Sorcerer 2 and 3 `2 × (4 + CON 1) = 10`; Warlock 2
  `(5 + CON 1) = 6`; total `7 + 6 + 10 + 6 = 29`.
- Draconic Resilience is gained at Sorcerer 3: `+3`. There is no later
  Sorcerer level in this build, so its later-level clause adds nothing.
- Expected maximum HP: `29 + 3 = 32`.

AC derivation:

- DEX 14 gives `+2`; final CHA 17 gives `+3`.
- Expected unarmored Draconic Resilience AC: `10 + 2 + 3 = 15`.
