# Oath of Domination

Status: **provisional, ready for design review only**. This is a MINT-FREE,
docs-only design on the parallel homebrew track. Every designed rule remains
subject to owner approval and playtest.

## 1. Identity

OWNER-APPROVAL: Identity paragraph and ancestry disclosure.

An Oath of Domination paladin speaks the future they believe they were shown
into the present, compelling enemies and arranging allies until the scene
matches that certainty. The oath does not spare its bearer grief, exhaustion,
or horror; it only denies that any of those costs could make the chosen course
wrong. Its repeated visible verb is **command**: in combat the paladin trades
their next strike of divine force for another order, in exploration they seek
and interpret signs, and in social scenes they make obedience sound like the
only remaining future.

The subclass's ancestry is concept-level only; no wording or mechanical form
from any non-SRD ancestry source appears here.

## 2. Tenets of Domination

The following tenets reproduce the binding creative inputs verbatim.

1. Every Tyrant Believes He Is the Exception. I have carved that
   warning into my own oath, and I have drawn the sword anyway. Let it
   be recorded that I knew.
2. On the Doubt of Counselors. Those who counsel me to wait are not
   disloyal - they have not been shown what I have been shown, and no
   grief of mine can lend them the sight I did not ask for. I have
   learned to nod at their counsel and walk past it, and the walking
   past does not get any easier the thousandth time.
3. On the Doubt of the Governed. A frightened people will always beg
   its protector to promise less, and I have heard that begging enough
   times now that it wakes me some nights on its own. Their fear is not
   their wisdom. Knowing that does not make it any easier to close the
   door on it.
4. On the Doubt of Companions. Those nearest me sometimes ask, gently,
   whether I have grown too sure, and it is the gentleness that does
   the cutting, not the question. I love them for asking. I have never
   once let the loving change what happens after, and some nights that
   is the whole of what will not let me sleep.
5. The Vision Does Not Rest. It speaks in the sign that comforts and
   the sign that warns, in the same relentless voice, and asks only
   that I keep listening long after I am too tired to want to. I have
   not found one sign that quarreled with another - I no longer have
   strength left over to hope that I might.
6. The Weight I Do Not Share. The throne does not answer to the ones
   it shelters - it only shelters them, and some days the not-answering
   is the heaviest part of the work. I gave up waiting for their thanks
   a long time ago. I have not given up wanting it, quietly, on the
   nights the weight sits on my chest and will not move.
7. One Throne, One Fault. I have made myself the only one who decides,
   so the fault will find one door and one name on it, and both are
   mine. I write that name into the account myself, by hand, every year
   it comes and finds nothing new to charge me for - and the writing
   never once gets lighter.
8. The Storm Some Called Someone Else's. Those who watched the ruin
   gather and called it none of their business have no claim on judging
   what I raised to stop it, and I do not pretend that not needing
   their permission has cost me nothing. I carry that too, alone, and
   it is heavier than being hated would be.
9. Every Stone Is Proof. The ruin recedes a little further with each
   wall I set against it, and it has receded so long now that I have
   forgotten what its opposite would even look like - and some nights
   that forgetting is its own kind of grief, a memory I gave up so the
   walls could stand. I do not thank anything for it anymore. I only
   keep building.

## 4. Subclass Features

The feature schedule is 3, 7, 15, and 20, matching the SRD paladin schedule
([SRD 5.2.1, Paladin Features](../srd/full/srd-5.2.1.txt#L3211) and
[Oath of Devotion](../srd/full/srd-5.2.1.txt#L3364)). Oath spell access and one
Channel Divinity option form the two-feature level-3 bundle; later levels each
grant one feature.

### Level 3: Voice of Domination

OWNER-APPROVAL: Voice of Domination rules text, including its costs, duration,
two-success ending condition, and concentration requirement.

Immediately after you cast *Command* using a level 1 spell slot and target
exactly one creature with it, you can expend one use of your Channel Divinity
(no action required) to make that creature the subject of your Voice of
Domination. The creature's saving throw against that casting counts for this
feature.

For up to 1 minute, you can take a Bonus Action on each of your subsequent
turns to cast *Command* without expending a spell slot, targeting only that
creature. You choose the command anew with each casting, and the target makes
the spell's saving throw as normal each time. You must be able to see the
target, and it must be within the spell's range when you cast the spell.

This effect requires Concentration as if you were concentrating on a spell.
It ends early when the target has succeeded on two saving throws against
castings of *Command* connected to this activation, including the casting that
initiated it; when you lose Concentration; or when you use this feature on
another creature. Successful saving throws need not be consecutive.

### Level 7: Aura of Certainty

OWNER-APPROVAL: Aura of Certainty rules text, including the language ribbon
and its interaction boundary.

While a creature that knows at least one language is in your Aura of
Protection, it understands the literal meaning of any words you speak in a
language you know. This understanding doesn't allow you to understand the
creature in return.

In addition, you and your allies have Advantage on saving throws to avoid or
end the Charmed condition while in your Aura of Protection.

OWNER-APPROVAL: Faithful Steed flavor line; it changes no rule.

> A paladin of this oath might call the steed granted by Faithful Steed the
> first creature to bend the knee.

### Level 15: Foreseen Formation

OWNER-APPROVAL: Foreseen Formation rules text, including its trigger, maximum
target count, movement, and Channel Divinity cost.

When an enemy you can see within 60 feet of yourself succeeds on a saving throw
against *Command* or another spell from your Oath of Domination Spells feature
that you cast, you can take a Reaction and expend one use of your Channel
Divinity. Choose a number of creatures up to your Charisma modifier (minimum of
one) from among yourself and your allies in your Aura of Protection. Each
chosen creature can immediately move up to half its Speed without provoking
Opportunity Attacks.

### Level 20: Dominion Foretold

OWNER-APPROVAL: Dominion Foretold rules text, including its one-minute
duration, 60-foot Truesight, Voice activation, removal of concentration and the
success limit, Charm protection, use cadence, and reload cost.

As a Bonus Action, you embody the dominion you believe was foretold, gaining
the following benefits for 1 minute.

**Foreseen Sight.** You have Truesight with a range of 60 feet.

**Irrefutable Voice.** When you activate this feature, choose one creature you
can see within 60 feet of yourself. As part of the same Bonus Action, you cast
*Command* without expending a spell slot, targeting only that creature. This
casting activates your Voice of Domination against the target without
expending Channel Divinity or a spell slot. Until Dominion Foretold ends, that
activation of Voice of Domination doesn't require Concentration and doesn't
end as a result of the target succeeding on saving throws. The target still
makes a fresh saving throw against every casting of *Command*.

**Unbroken Purpose.** You and your allies have Immunity to the Charmed
condition while in your Aura of Protection. If a Charmed ally enters the aura,
that condition has no effect on that ally while there
([SRD aura wording](../srd/full/srd-5.2.1.txt#L3417)).

You can end these benefits early (no action required). That activation of Voice
of Domination ends when Dominion Foretold ends.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest.
You can also restore your use of it by expending a level 5 spell slot (no action
required).

## 5. Power-Budget Worksheet

### Host posture and protected dial

The host is a paladin with **low-medium subclass dependence**: the base class
already supplies strikes, healing, spells, Channel Divinity, and Aura of
Protection. The subclass therefore adds fixed spell access, one Channel
Divinity mode, restrained aura support, a resource-priced reposition, and a
short transformation. It adds no damage dice, attack, Action, saving-throw DC
bonus, companion, or subclass-only use pool. This follows the paladin posture
in [guideline 01](subclass-guidelines/01-power-budget.md#class-dependence-and-power-shape).

The identity-bearing dial is the repeated Bonus Action casting of *Command*.
Its balancing dials are the initial level 1 slot, Channel Divinity use,
Concentration, one fixed target, normal 60-foot spell range, fresh saves, two
successful saves ending the activation, and competition with Divine Smite and
other paladin Bonus Actions.

### Explicit numerical checks

**Voice uptime.** If a target has a 50% chance to succeed on each save, the
uncapped negative-binomial expectation before the second success is 4 total
castings and 2 failed saves. At a 65% success chance, it is about 3.08 castings
and 1.08 failed saves; at a 35% success chance, it is about 5.71 castings and
3.71 failed saves. The one-minute ceiling, range, visibility, Concentration,
and Bonus Action cost can shorten all three cases. Each failed save normally
costs the target one turn under *Command*, so the two-success threshold is the
most sensitive entry-tier control number.

**Sacred Weapon comparison.** At level 5, assuming two attacks, a normal 65%
hit chance, Charisma +3, and a 1d8 weapon with Strength +4, Sacred Weapon's
approximate accuracy gain is 15 percentage points per attack. That is about
`2 × 0.15 × 8.5 = 2.55` added expected damage each round before other hit
riders, for up to 10 minutes and without Concentration. Voice adds `0` damage,
and on every recast round its Bonus Action cannot also carry Divine Smite.

**Formation ceiling.** At Charisma +5, Foreseen Formation can move five
eligible creatures up to half their individual Speeds. With five Speed-30
creatures, the extreme collective movement is 75 feet, but all five must begin
inside the 10-foot aura at level 15 (30 feet at level 18), the trigger must
occur, the paladin must spend a Reaction and Channel Divinity, and no creature
gains an extra action, attack, or damage rider.

**Capstone comparison.** At level 20, Holy Nimbus can deal Charisma modifier
plus Proficiency Bonus damage—normally `5 + 6 = 11`—to each enemy that starts
each turn in the aura for up to 10 minutes. Dominion Foretold lasts 1 minute,
deals 0 automatic damage, and addresses one fixed target. It buys reliability
through slotless attempts rather than no-save success: every attempt still
permits a saving throw.

### Action economy and resource stress

- **Ordinary turn:** Attack action; Bonus Action available for Divine Smite,
  Lay On Hands, Divine Sense, or another base option; Reaction available;
  no subclass Concentration or rider.
- **Voice activation turn:** Action and a level 1 slot cast *Command*; Channel
  Divinity is expended with no added action; Bonus Action remains unused by the
  feature; Voice becomes the one tracked state.
- **Voice recast turn:** Attack action; Bonus Action casts slotless *Command*;
  Reaction remains available; Concentration belongs to Voice; Divine Smite
  cannot use the same Bonus Action.
- **Burst turn at 20:** Dominion Foretold's Bonus Action includes its first
  *Command*; the Action can be Attack, but that turn's Bonus Action is already
  spent, so Divine Smite cannot be added.
- **One-fight day:** Channel Divinity and spell slots can be concentrated into
  the encounter, but only one Voice is active at a time, each command gets a
  save, and the subclass creates no extra damage carrier.
- **No-Short-Rest day:** two Channel Divinity uses at entry and three from
  Paladin 11 remain available after the Long Rest; the oath spells and aura
  still function when those uses are gone.
- **Rest-rich day:** each Short Rest restores only one native Channel Divinity
  use. Nothing in the subclass restores its own input or removes the initial
  spell-slot price of ordinary Voice activations.

## 8. The Vision

OWNER-APPROVAL: Final prose of the written vision and its three stated readings.

> At the foot of a pale tower, a crowned figure sets the final stone in a wall
> around a sleeping city. Beyond the wall, black water climbs though no rain
> falls. At dawn the water is gone, the crown lies beneath the final stone, and
> every door in the city stands open.

The passage deliberately supports at least three readings:

1. **The paladin's reading:** the wall must be completed, its ruler must accept
   the cost, and the coming ruin will be turned away.
2. **The darker reading:** setting the final stone makes the water rise; by
   dawn the ruler is buried in the work and the open city is empty, conquered,
   or dead.
3. **The benign reading:** the water recedes at dawn without intervention, the
   crown is willingly laid down, and the open doors mark a city that never
   needed a wall.

The vision supplies images and sequence but no causal words. The paladin adds
causality, treats that reading as the only one, and never audits the addition.

OWNER-APPROVAL: Provisional resolution of D186's tenet-title mismatch.

D186 assigns this refusal to re-examine the vision to a tenet titled “What Was
Shown Needs No Second Showing,” but no tenet with that title exists in the
binding final set reproduced in section 2. This draft does not invent or
rewrite a tenth tenet. Pending owner confirmation, **The Vision Does Not Rest**
carries that function: the paladin reads every comforting or warning sign in
one voice and finds no sign that quarrels with another.

## 9. DM Guidance

The following paragraph reproduces the binding guidance:

> The toll lives entirely in the body and the room - hands steadied by
> long practice a beat before he signs the order, insomnia no one
> explains, a nightly vow spoken alone that would sound like begging to
> anyone who overheard it - and NEVER in the timeline of his choices. The
> instant hesitation delays a decision or reopens something settled, the
> frame breaks: the shaking is real, but the hand still fires, every
> time, on schedule. Dissenters get their strongest argument; he agrees
> it deserves an answer, grieves it, and changes nothing. Outcomes must
> always read two ways (saved town / massacred rebellion). He never
> supplies the reader's doubt himself; the world never confirms or
> debunks the vision (Augury and Divination answer ambiguously by rule -
> D182).

OWNER-APPROVAL: Added DM guidance for using the vision, consent, and rules
adjudication.

- Secretly choose one reading, change which reading seems strongest as events
  develop, or never choose. Preserve the distinction between a true image and
  a fallible interpretation; ambiguity is not permission to make *Augury* or
  *Divination* knowingly false.
- Put the bodily toll after the decision or beside its execution, never before
  it as a pause that could change the outcome. Let witnesses disagree about
  whether the result was rescue or conquest.
- Discuss coercive magic, authoritarian player-character conduct, and
  player-versus-player boundaries before play. The oath's certainty is not
  permission to remove another player's agency outside the game's agreed
  rules.
- *Command* cast through Voice of Domination retains the spell's listed command
  options, range, and saving throw. A new casting creates a new spell effect;
  it does not extend a failed command beyond that target's next turn.
- Record Voice with one notation: target name followed by zero, one, or two
  successful saves. For Dominion Foretold, mark the same state as unlimited by
  successes until the transformation ends.
