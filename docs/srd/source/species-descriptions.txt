NINE species, and that is the complete set. Dragonborn, Dwarf, Elf, Gnome,
Goliath, Halfling, Human, Orc, Tiefling. Aasimar is NOT in SRD 5.2.1 (zero
occurrences in the whole document), despite appearing in the 2024 Player's
Handbook. Checked, because assuming ten and quietly shipping nine is the
failure this directory exists to prevent.

five, and any parser written against the file would have been provably correct
