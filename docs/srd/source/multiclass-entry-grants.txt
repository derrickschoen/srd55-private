--- Verbatim extracts: the "As a Multiclass Character" clause of all twelve
    classes. ---

WHY THIS MATTERS. multiclassing.txt records that a character entering a SECOND
class gains "only SOME of the new class's starting proficiencies". These twelve
clauses are that subset, per class. Computing a multiclass sheet from a class's
FULL Core Traits row would OVER-GRANT — most classes give their skill
proficiencies and saving throws only to the character who STARTED in them.

LAYOUT: two-column. The right column begins at a DIFFERENT offset per class, so
each slice below is calibrated to where that class's own heading starts rather
than to one fixed column. Nothing is edited out.
