FOUR backgrounds, and that is the complete set: Acolyte, Criminal, Sage,
Soldier. The 2024 Player's Handbook prints sixteen; SRD 5.2.1 licenses four.
Three independent checks agree, and all three are in this document rather than
in anybody's memory:

  - `grep -c 'Ability Scores:'` over the whole 24,074-line extraction returns
    exactly 4, and so does `grep -cE '^ *Feat: '`;
  - the table of contents lists Acolyte, Criminal, Sage and Soldier under
    `Character Backgrounds ... 83` and nothing else;
  - chapter 1's own Ability Scores and Backgrounds table names only Soldier
    and Acolyte, and no fifth background appears anywhere.

Hand-typing the other twelve would be inventing content the licence does not
cover, which is a worse failure than shipping four.
