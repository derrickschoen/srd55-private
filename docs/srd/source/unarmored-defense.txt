The two features differ in BOTH the second ability and the shield clause, and
each difference is load-bearing for the Armor Class resolver: the Barbarian
formula stays eligible while a Shield is held (allows_shield = true); the
Monk formula is broken by wielding one (allows_shield = false, D75).

=== Barbarian (page 29) ===

Level 1: Unarmored Defense
While you aren’t wearing any armor, your base Armor Class equals 10 plus
your Dexterity and Constitution modifiers. You can use a Shield and still
gain this benefit.

=== Monk (page 50) ===

Level 1: Unarmored Defense
While you aren’t wearing armor or wielding a Shield, your base Armor Class
equals 10 plus your Dexterity and Wisdom modifiers.
