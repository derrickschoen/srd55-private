--- Verbatim extract: SRD 5.2.1 Background Descriptions (page ~82). ---

FOUR backgrounds, and that is the COMPLETE set: Acolyte, Criminal, Sage,
Soldier. The 2024 Player's Handbook has sixteen; the SRD has these four.
Counted from 'Feat:' occurrences in the chapter, then read back by name.
Anything beyond these four is NOT bundleable content.

Each grants three Ability Scores, an origin feat, two Skill Proficiencies, a
Tool Proficiency and an equipment choice. Two grant Magic Initiate, which
grants SPELLS — that is the existing grant-rule and source-instance
machinery, not a new path.

LAYOUT: two-column, sliced by CHARACTER at the column boundary (59) so no
multi-byte quote is split and no value is truncated mid-word. Acolyte and
Criminal are in the LEFT column, Sage and Soldier in the RIGHT.
