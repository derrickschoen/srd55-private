These two invocations are why the Extra Attack model cannot be keyed on
(class, level) alone. Thirsting Blade grants the feature from an INVOCATION,
not a class table, and grants it FOR ONE WEAPON ONLY. Devouring Blade then
upgrades that same grant to two extra attacks.

=== Thirsting Blade (Eldritch Invocation) ===

       Thirsting Blade

       Prerequisite: Level 5+ Warlock, Pact of the Blade

       Invocation

       You gain the Extra Attack feature for your pact
       weapon only. With that feature, you can attack
       twice with the weapon instead of once when you
       take the Attack action on your turn.
