# Player's one-page table guide

Keep this beside the shared tabletop during play. You do not need to learn the controls behind the DM's screen.

## Read the board

- Tokens are the creatures and objects whose positions matter. Your named token is your character. A bright or outlined token is usually the creature whose turn or choice is active.
- Fog is what your character cannot currently see. A fogged square is unknown, not empty; wait for the DM to reveal it.
- Marked areas show something affecting part of the board, such as a spell area, difficult ground, or a lasting surface. Ask the DM what a mark means before moving through it if the effect is not clear.
- Tell the DM where you want to move and what you want to do. When the VTT presents "Legal choices", choose the action and then "Confirm choice"; for a reaction, the immediate buttons are "Use reaction" and "Decline reaction".

## Watch the decision tray

The DM's "Decision tray" collects choices that need an answer before play moves on, including reactions and death saving throws. The DM may ask you aloud and make the matching choice for you.

Each reaction can use one of three preferences for the encounter:

- "ask" means pause for your answer.
- "always" means use that reaction automatically when the offered trigger is valid.
- "never" means decline it automatically.

Tell the DM whenever you want to change a preference. "Always" is permission to spend the reaction when offered, not a promise that every trigger will happen.

## Use the dice log

The "Shared dice log" keeps the rolls made at the table. Read the newest entry when you need the die results and total. If the DM asks for a roll, agree on what is being rolled before pressing "Roll dice" so the entry is understandable to everyone.

## Between rooms

Your character does not reset when a room ends. Current Hit Points, spent spell slots, used features, and other resources carry into the next room.

When the DM offers "Short Rest before next room", say whether you want to spend Hit Point Dice and how many. The DM records the choices and uses "Take Short Rest and enter next room". A short rest applies only the healing and feature recovery your character qualifies for; anything not recovered stays spent.

## At 0 Hit Points

At 0 Hit Points, the VTT can ask for your death saving throw through the decision tray. The DM may use "Roll Death Save for [character name]" for you. Your shared view will still show that a death save was resolved, but the DM can hide the roll number and result from players. That is intentional table suspense, not a missing roll. Keep making choices only when the DM or your view asks for them.
