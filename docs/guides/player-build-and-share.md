# Build and share your 2024 character

Use the public app address your DM sends you. Your character is saved in the browser on your device, so finish the build and make the share link on that same device.

## Build the example Fighter

1. Open the app. On the "Characters" page, choose "Create a character".
2. Under "Choose a class", choose "Fighter". Enter your name in "Character name", then choose "Create character".
3. On "Set ability scores", leave "Standard array" selected for this example and choose "Set ability scores".
4. Under "Choose a species", choose "Choose Dwarf".
5. Under "Choose a background", select "Soldier". Keep the suggested ability increases and the displayed Origin feat, then choose "Apply background".
6. Under "Choose skills", select "Perception" in "Fighter skill 1" and choose "Choose Fighter skill 1". Select "Survival" in "Fighter skill 2" and choose "Choose Fighter skill 2".
7. On "Confirm starting equipment", find "Class package — Fighter" and choose "Take option A". Find "Background package — Soldier" and choose "Take this package".
8. In "Required Fighter choices", select "Archery" from "Fighting Style" and choose "Choose Fighting Style". Under "Weapon Mastery", choose "Choose mastery: Flail", "Choose mastery: Greatsword", and "Choose mastery: Javelin".
9. Wait for the message beginning "Both equipment packages are recorded. Every guided level 1 step is complete." Then choose "Back to characters".

## Review the sheet

1. On your character card, choose "Open workspace".
2. Choose "Character sheet" and review the page headed "Character sheet —" followed by your character's name. Check that the sheet matches the choices you just made.
3. If something needs changing, choose "Open planner". When the sheet is ready, choose "All characters".

## Make and send the link

1. On your character card, choose "Share [your character name] by link".
2. The three "Include" checkboxes start off. Leave them off unless you intend to share warning acknowledgements, loadouts, or the written text named beside the checkbox.
3. Choose "Create share link". When "Generated character share link" appears, choose "Copy link".
4. Paste the link into your normal message to the DM. Keep this browser's character: the link sends a copy; it does not move your original.

## DM: import the character and select it for the party

1. Open the player's link. The app shows a "Shared character preview" without importing anything yet.
2. Review the character name and summary, then choose "Add to my characters". The status confirms that the character "was added" and offers "Open character".
3. Open the app's `/vtt` page and choose "Compose a rules encounter from stored characters".
4. Under "Stored characters", select the checkbox labeled "[character name] — Fighter 1". Select two to four other stored characters as well; the composer accepts three to five.
5. Choose "Start DM encounter" when the party is ready.
