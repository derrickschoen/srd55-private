# Oath of the Long Grudge

**Visibility:** `ui_hidden`

**Intended content kind / parent:** compact subclass fixture; Paladin parent.
Only the bonded-rider feature is recovered, not a complete oath.

## Mechanic facts

- The feature bonds one target and adds damage to that bonded target on the
  first hit that qualifies each turn.
- The rider is one die: d6 at level 5, d8 at level 11, and d10 at level 17.
- A critical hit doubles the rider dice.
- The validator carried the bond for ten rounds and exercised two attacks per
  turn. The ten-round value is a validation assumption until rules duration is
  supplied; the attack count belongs to the test chassis.

## Required structured effects

- Target-bound state with an explicit target identity and lifetime.
- A once-per-turn, on-hit damage rider gated by that target identity.
- Level-scaled rider die and critical-hit dice doubling.

## Deliberately unsupported mechanics

- Base weapon damage and other Paladin damage features are outside this fixture.
- No unrecovered oath features, spell list, or narrative package are invented.
- Bond application and replacement are not inferred from the validator's
  preloaded bond.

## MISSING FACTS

- Feature acquisition level and any other subclass schedule entries.
- Bond application action, range, valid targets, duration, uses, recovery, and
  replacement rules.
- Rider damage type and qualifying attack restrictions.
- Whether the recovered level-5/11/17 die steps are the authored scaling points
  or validation snapshots of a different progression.
