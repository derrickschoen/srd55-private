# Anchor Point

**Visibility:** `ui_hidden`

**Intended content kind / parent:** compact feat fixture; no parent class. The
preserved validator explicitly identifies the mechanic as a feat.

## Mechanic facts

- On the first qualifying hit each turn, the target takes a damage rider, has
  Speed 0, and has its reactions locked for that turn.
- The rider is 1d8 at levels 5 and 11, then 2d8 at level 17.
- A critical hit doubles the rider dice.
- The steady Fighter attack counts used for validation are chassis inputs, not
  feat requirements.

## Required structured effects

- A once-per-turn, first-hit damage rider with level scaling and critical dice
  doubling.
- A Speed-0 control effect tied to the same successful hit.
- A reaction lock tied to the same successful hit.
- Explicit duration/end timing for both control effects before publication.

## Deliberately unsupported mechanics

- Opportunity-attack damage is not part of the fixture or its damage result.
- Action Surge and the validator's separate four-attack diagnostic are not part
  of the authored mechanic.
- Base attack damage is outside the fixture.

## MISSING FACTS

- Feat category, prerequisites, repeatability, and any ability-score increase.
- Qualifying attack or weapon restrictions and rider damage type.
- Exact duration/end timing for Speed 0 and the reaction lock.
- Whether either control effect permits a save, has an immunity, or applies to
  only particular reactions.
- Authoritative scaling points behind the recovered level-5/11/17 snapshots.
