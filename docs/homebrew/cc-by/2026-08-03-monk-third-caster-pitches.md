# Monk third-caster subclass — pitch catalog (D169)

Status: owner browsing. Nothing here is drafted rules text; each pitch is a
fantasy + delivery hook. The chosen pitch goes through the full
`docs/design/subclass-guidelines/` seven-step process into a draft document
(like the Oath of Domination), with the distance checklist re-run against
the final mechanics.

Compiled by the supervisor from three independent clean-room brainstorm
panels (Fable, opus, sonnet) plus two owner-directed flavor rounds. All
spell names verified present in SRD 5.2.1.

## Chassis (fixed for every pitch)

- 2024 monk subclass schedule: levels **3 / 6 / 11 / 17**, SRD cadence
  3×1, 6×1, 11×1, 17×1 (a spellcasting grant makes level 3 a 3×2 bundle,
  legitimized by the Bard/Cleric/Paladin corpus pattern).
- Third-caster slot progression: 1st-level slots at 3, 2nd at 7, 3rd at 13,
  4th at 19. Exists to exercise the app's third-caster machinery (D169
  replaces Eldritch Knight / Arcane Trickster, which are not in the SRD).
- **Wisdom** casting (guideline 01 heuristic 12: no new ability dependency).
- Monk dependence is **Medium** (guideline 01): the subclass should alter
  what Focus buys — movement, control, utility — not stack damage riders.
- The monk Bonus Action is crowded (Flurry / Patient Defense / Step of the
  Wind): casting mostly lives on the Action or rides existing attacks.
- Open-content ancestor precedent: the 3.5 SRD "Psionic Fist" prestige
  class (a monk-caster is an established open-content idea).

## Distance from existing work

Isolated-researcher findings (names are facts; shapes only, no reproduced
text):

- **"Warrior of the Mystic Arts"** (*Arcana Unleashed*, upcoming official
  WotC, all rights reserved): EK-style spell slots grafted onto the monk
  with an attack-replacement casting gimmick lifted from the Fighter
  pattern — already flagged by reviewers as mismatched to the monk's attack
  economy.
- **"Way of the Arcane Hand"** (Drakkenheim-community homebrew, unlicensed):
  real slots + **ki-spent-to-upcast capped by proficiency** +
  **concentration-gated bonus damage on strikes** + an anti-spell capstone.
- Crowded neighbors: Four Elements (ki-as-spell-points menu with hard
  tension vs core ki features), Astral Self / Sun Soul (arcane-flavored
  non-casters), and a recurring "Arcane Fist/Hand" homebrew name pattern.

### How this catalog avoids them — three rules applied to every pitch

1. **List distance.** Both references (and essentially all caster-monk
   homebrew) borrow the **wizard/arcane** list. Most pitches below draw on
   **cleric / druid / ranger / bard slices — Wisdom lists for a Wisdom
   class** — or on school slices (illusion, divination) used through a
   non-arcane delivery. Nobody has shipped a divine/primal third-caster
   monk.
2. **Delivery distance.** The banned mechanical signatures are:
   ki/Focus-to-upcast; concentration-scaled melee damage;
   attack-replaced-by-cantrip; core-resource-buys-spell-menu as the primary
   engine. **No pitch below uses any of them.** Each pitch's originality
   lives in a delivery gimmick neither reference has: delayed seals,
   miss-triggered casting, ground-marking, duplicate-participation,
   mid-fight oaths, stun-extension, and so on.
3. **Name distance.** The crowded template is "Way of the
   [Arcane/Mystic/Astral] ___" and "Arcane"+body-part. All names below use
   the SRD 2024 house style (**Warrior of the …**) with non-arcane nouns.

## The pitches

### Mirror & illusion family

1. **Warrior of the Ten Selves** — discipline so complete it overflows into
   copies. Mirror Image as signature: duplicates are stances you left
   standing — a flurry can land from where a duplicate stands, and a
   shattering image throws one last strike. *Distance: neither reference
   touches illusion or duplicates; the engine is positional, not
   slot-conversion.*
2. **Warrior of the Empty Reflection** — the mirror that fights back:
   illusions replay an enemy's own last move; the more perfect the copy,
   the harder it lands. Duelist identity.
3. **Warrior of the Painted World** — trained inside illusions until the
   world can't tell the difference: a strike makes a painted thing briefly
   true (the illusory bridge holds one crossing).
4. **Warrior of the Unseen Palm** — Invisibility/Mislead approach; the
   reveal is the strike.
5. **Warrior of the Still Point** — you move; a motionless you remains.
   Spells originate from either; swap once per turn. Most striking image;
   highest bookkeeping risk (second tracked position).

### Mockery & voice family (bard list)

6. **Warrior of the Laughing Court** — the jester-ascetic: the insult is a
   pressure point. Vicious Mockery weaves between punches; Hideous Laughter
   is the finisher — the enemy who finally gets the joke can't stand up.
   *Distance: bard list + verbal delivery; no reference resembles it.*
7. **Warrior of the Barbed Tongue** — the dueling taunt made sacred: your
   mockery marks a target that fights worse against everyone but you.
   Bane, Vicious Mockery, Fear. Protection by provocation.
8. **Warrior of the Stolen Face** — mimicry as martial art: an enemy's own
   words in their own voice; a borrowed face in negotiation (Disguise Self,
   Charm Person); copied gestures predict theirs in combat.

### Lockdown family (the Stunning Strike extension)

9. **Warrior of the Closed Gate** — cleric/paladin slice; the nerve-sealer.
   Command at range ("halt", "drop", "kneel"); signature: a landed Stunning
   Strike can be extended by a slot into a hold — the spell deepens the
   stun the class already landed rather than opening a second lock. Hold
   Person arrives at 7, two levels after Stunning Strike at 5.
   *Distance: divine list; the interlock keys off the class's own level-5
   feature — no reference has anything shaped like it.*
10. **Warrior of the Hundred Knots** — druid/ranger primal bindings:
    Entangle, Ensnaring Strike, Spike Growth. Strikes tie limbs; ground
    grows rope; speed 0 instead of stunned. The grappler who never grabs.

### Divine & primal list family

11. **Warrior of the Quiet Temple** — cleric list; the temple guardian.
    Sanctuary/Bless at 3, Zone of Truth at 7, and at 13 **Spirit Guardians
    swirling around a flurry** — the monk is the ward. Social presence:
    the officiant who makes a room tell the truth.
12. **Warrior of the Rooted Mountain** — druid list; immovability made
    literal. Barkskin, Meld into Stone, Entangle: Patient Defense roots
    you, the ground grabs, nothing moves you. *The anti-mobility monk — an
    inversion of the class's own axis, so it reads as new rather than
    stacked.*
13. **Warrior of the Patient Hunt** — ranger list; Hunter's Mark riding
    monk speed, Ensnaring Strike on an unarmed hit. Run-down specialist;
    tightest damage-budget squeeze of the catalog.
14. **Warrior of the Spoken Vow** — paladin list; mid-fight oaths binding
    both sides ("none pass me" — the benefit lasts while you keep it).
    Command, Shield of Faith, Zone of Truth. Rhymes with the Oath of
    Domination if the two homebrews should feel related.

### Panel leaders (mechanics-first standouts)

15. **Warrior of the Settling Dust** *(opus #1)* — your punch marks the
    ground; your area spell lands on the mark from any distance and never
    catches you inside it. Best budget fit (control, medium-low damage);
    rides the Attack action; obvious 6/11/17 upgrades with no new
    subsystem.
16. **The Broken Guard** *(sonnet wildcard)* — once per turn a **missed**
    unarmed strike may become a spell instead; the whiff is the somatic
    component. *The only miss-triggered caster anywhere in the researched
    space — uncopyable from either reference by construction.*
17. **Seal synthesis** — all three panels converged independently on
    seal/ward delivery: strikes or touches plant a held seal (a ward on an
    ally, a delayed spell on an enemy) that discharges on a later trigger.
    Preparation-based rather than conversion-based — the maximal distance
    from ki-to-upcast.
18. **Warrior of the Sealed Word** *(Fable)* — the calligrapher variant of
    17: spells prepared as inked seals on skin, weapons, doorframes;
    non-combat presence through wards and old script.
19. **Warrior of the Tidal Breath** *(Fable)* — breath-cycle rhythm: after
    a spell, the next Flurry costs no Focus; after a Flurry, the next
    spell's movement rides Step of the Wind. Alternation, not stacking —
    cadence is the engine, not currency conversion.
20. **Warrior of the Borrowed Sky** *(Fable)* — magic only works in motion:
    moving 15+ feet before casting upgrades the spell. Map-as-resource;
    needs a baseline for cramped rooms.

## Cross-cutting fusion notes

- **Closed Gate + Quiet Temple** merge into the strongest single "divine
  monk" package (Command extends stuns *and* Spirit Guardians ride the
  flurry) — the guidelines would force one of the two hooks to be the
  level-3 engine, the other arriving at 11.
- **Ten Selves + Laughing Court** fuse into a mocker whose duplicates
  deliver the insults — one engine must win.

## Next step

Owner picks 1-3 pitches for a bake-off: each gets a drafted level-3 bundle
(spellcasting grant + engine feature in SRD register) side by side, then
one winner goes through the full guideline process (fantasy sentence, three
scene moments, budget worksheet, failure-taxonomy pass, 05 checklist,
distance checklist re-run) into the draft document for owner approval.
