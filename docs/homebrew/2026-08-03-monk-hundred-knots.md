# Warrior of the Hundred Knots

Status: **provisional, ready for design review only**. This is a MINT-FREE,
docs-only full subclass draft. The frozen bake-off inputs are reproduced
without mechanical change; authored text remains subject to owner approval and
playtest.

## 1. Identity

OWNER-APPROVAL: Identity paragraph and ancestry disclosure.

A Warrior of the Hundred Knots draws living bindings through strikes and
surrounding growth in order to close the routes by which a chosen quarry could
escape. Its repeated visible verb is **bind**: in combat the monk catches a
concentrated quarry in a slowing strike, in exploration the monk follows tracks
and closes a route with growth or wind, and in social scenes the monk asks a
local Beast what crossed its territory and turns that testimony into a trail.

The 3.5 SRD Psionic Fist is used only as open-content, concept-level precedent
for the frame of a monk who develops supernatural casting; no wording or
mechanical form is taken from it.

**Cantrips.** You know the cantrips shown on the Hundred Knots curated list.

**Prepared Spells.** You always have the spells in the Hundred Knots curated
list prepared when you reach the Monk levels shown there. These spells count as
Monk spells for you.

**Spell Slots.** The Monk Third-Caster Spell Slots table shows how many spell
slots you have to cast your level 1+ Monk spells. To cast one of those spells,
you must expend a slot of the spell's level or higher. You regain all expended
spell slots when you finish a Long Rest.

**Spellcasting Ability.** Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for your Monk
spells. Your spell save DC equals 8 plus your Wisdom modifier and Proficiency
Bonus, and your spell attack modifier equals your Wisdom modifier plus your
Proficiency Bonus.

## 3. Subclass Features

The substantive feature schedule is 3, 6, 11, and 17, matching the SRD Monk
and Warrior of the Open Hand schedule ([SRD Monk table](../srd/full/srd-5.2.1.txt#L3022)
and [Open Hand](../srd/full/srd-5.2.1.txt#L3130)). Spellcasting is compact
support beside the level-3 engine.

### Level 3: Verdant Bindings

FROZEN BAKE-OFF ENGINE: reproduced without mechanical change.

Your Unarmed Strikes count as weapons for the purpose of casting Ensnaring
Strike.

Once on each of your turns when you hit a creature with an Unarmed Strike, you
can reduce its Speed by 10 feet until the start of your next turn if either of
the following is true:

- the creature is the target of a spell on which you are concentrating; or
- the creature occupies Difficult Terrain created by a spell on which you are
  concentrating.

Immediately after you reduce a creature's Speed in this way, you can expend 1
Focus Point to tighten the binding. The creature must succeed on a Strength
saving throw against your Focus Point save DC or have a Speed of 0 until the
start of your next turn. On a successful save, the 10-foot reduction remains.

### Level 6: Spellwoven Flurry

OWNER-APPROVAL: Full rules text and Hundred Knots cantrip interaction note.

While you aren't holding a weapon, whenever you use Flurry of Blows, you can
replace one of the Unarmed Strikes it grants with casting a cantrip you know
that has a casting time of an action. The cantrip is cast as part of the Bonus
Action you take to use Flurry of Blows, and you can't replace more than one
Unarmed Strike in this way each time you use that Bonus Action.

For this subclass, *Produce Flame* is the signature combat interaction. It can
replace one Flurry strike, but it isn't an Unarmed Strike and therefore can't
trigger Verdant Bindings. *Druidcraft* and, from Monk level 10, *Guidance* can
instead supply utility. No cantrip adds another strike or substitutes for the
binding's required hit.

### Level 11: Closing the Circle

OWNER-APPROVAL: Entire feature, including voluntary movement, Reaction,
60-foot range, Focus cost, and success/failure results.

When a creature you can see within 60 feet of yourself willingly moves into or
within Difficult Terrain created by a spell on which you are concentrating, you
can take a Reaction and expend 1 Focus Point to close its route. The creature
must make a Strength saving throw against your Focus Point save DC. On a failed
save, its Speed is 0 until the end of the current turn. On a successful save,
its Speed is reduced by 10 feet until the end of the current turn.

### Level 17: World-Knot

OWNER-APPROVAL: Entire feature, including the 3-Focus cost, teleportation
restriction, one-minute duration, concentration boundary, repeated save, and
one-target limit.

When you reduce a creature's Speed with Verdant Bindings, you can expend 3
Focus Points to tie a world-knot instead of expending 1 Focus Point to tighten
the binding. The creature must make a Strength saving throw against your Focus
Point save DC. On a failed save, its Speed is 0 and it can't teleport for 1
minute. At the end of each of its turns, it repeats the save, ending the effect
on itself on a success. On a successful initial save, only the 10-foot reduction
from Verdant Bindings remains.

When you tie the world-knot, choose one spell on which you are concentrating
that made the creature eligible for Verdant Bindings. The world-knot ends early
if your concentration on that spell ends. You can have only one creature bound
by this feature at a time; tying another world-knot ends the first.

## 4. Power-Budget Worksheet

### Host posture and protected dial

Monk subclass dependence is **Medium**. The subclass redirects concentration,
Unarmed Strikes, terrain, Reactions, and Focus toward movement control. It adds
no attack, action, damage rider, accuracy bonus, Armor Class, save-DC bonus, or
new currency. The protected dial is closing a quarry's route; concentration,
hits, Difficult Terrain, once-per-turn/Reaction timing, Strength saves,
duration, one-target limits, and Focus price that dial.

The list is a major grant: ten always-prepared level 1–4 spells and three fixed
cantrips, compared with no spell grant on Warrior of the Open Hand. The list
also contains real damage, especially *Hunter's Mark* and *Spike Growth*, so
the subclass features themselves deliberately add zero damage.

### Explicit numerical checks

- Against Speed 30, the baseline 10-foot reduction removes one-third of the
  target's movement for one round. Speed 40 loses one-quarter; no damage or
  accuracy is added. The 1-Focus stop permits a Strength save and keeps only the
  baseline on success.
- At level 5, *Hunter's Mark* across three attacks at a 65% hit chance adds
  about `3 × 0.65 × 3.5 = 6.83` expected damage if concentration survives. A
  four-attack Flurry turn raises that to about 9.10. Those numbers belong to the
  spell-list budget; Verdant Bindings adds 0 damage and the spell's Bonus Action
  competes with Martial Arts/Flurry.
- At level 6, a d8 + Dexterity +4 Unarmed Strike contributes about
  `0.65 × 8.5 = 5.53` expected damage. A 2d8 *Produce Flame* attack contributes
  about `0.65 × 9 = 5.85`, only 0.32 more, and cannot trigger Verdant Bindings.
- Closing the Circle can end at most one creature's voluntary movement per
  round because it consumes the Reaction. A successful save retains only a
  10-foot reduction for the current turn.
- A World-Knot delivered through Flurry costs at least 4 Focus total: 1 for
  Flurry and 3 for the knot. It adds no damage, requires the qualifying
  concentration to persist, allows an initial Strength save, and repeats that
  save after every failed-save turn.

### Action economy and resource stress

- **Ordinary martial turn:** Attack action; Bonus Action Martial Arts/Focus;
  Verdant Bindings can ride one eligible Unarmed hit.
- **Setup turn:** *Hunter's Mark* or *Ensnaring Strike* consumes the Bonus Action;
  *Entangle* or *Spike Growth* consumes the Action; all use concentration and
  compete with one another.
- **Flurry casting turn:** Attack action; weapon-free 1-Focus Flurry replaces
  one strike with a cantrip; remaining Unarmed hits can bind.
- **Route-control round:** Closing the Circle consumes the Reaction, leaving
  Deflect Attacks unavailable until the Reaction returns.
- **One-fight day:** slots and Focus can be concentrated; strongest capstone
  delivery costs at least 4 Focus and the engine never adds damage to the spell.
- **No-Short-Rest / rest-rich days:** cantrips and 10-foot bindings remain when
  Focus is scarce if slots/concentration remain; only native Focus recovers on a
  Short Rest, and no output restores an input.

## 7. Distance Checklist

The three pitch-catalog rules were re-run against the final mechanics.

1. **List distance — Pass.** The fixed list is a primal ranger/druid slice
   centered on quarry and terrain rather than a general arcane list.
2. **Delivery distance — Pass with D189's explicit exception.** Focus never
   raises a slot level or buys a spell menu. No subclass rule adds damage for
   maintaining concentration; *Hunter's Mark* and *Spike Growth* deal only
   their printed, budgeted spell damage. Spellwoven Flurry replaces one strike
   only inside the weapon-free, Focus-funded Bonus Action Flurry and never
   replaces an Attack-action attack. Bindings instead use Unarmed hits, spell
   terrain, Speed, and Strength saves.
3. **Name distance — Pass.** “Warrior of the Hundred Knots” follows the SRD
   Monk house style and uses no Arcane/Mystic/Astral or body-part template.
