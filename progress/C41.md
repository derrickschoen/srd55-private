## Delivered

- `UpdateAbilityCommand` validates the six abilities and inclusive 1–30
  boundary, stores the prior score, timestamps the persisted character row, and
  returns the exact unsigned inverse.
- The shared slot command layer enforces character ownership and locks before
  capturing the exact five-field restore state. Every successful mode writes a
  fresh timestamp and returns a character-bound HMAC-signed restore inverse.

  clears a prior override. Clear persists unselected active/discarded state.
  Keep-override requires a selection and trims its required note.
- Restore rejects inactive/missing spell versions. Active/override states are
  re-evaluated against current rules; newly orphaned slots remain orphaned with
  the exact rule/source preservation explanation.
- Seven integration tests assert stored characters/slots, timestamp changes,
  signed prior state and redo state, exact `CharacterState.diff()` audit inputs,
  byte-identical rejection paths, and stale-inverse revalidation.
