  cumulative class progressions, and combined static/cumulative subclass rules.

  config and class-level activation gates are evaluated against persisted sources.

  capability discovery; recursively granted sources; and the simplified
  per-character Wizard spellbook set.

  replacing IDs or selections. Removed rules and source trees retain their rows,
  selections, prior config, timestamps, and exact persisted invalid reasons.
- Preserved `kept_override`, refreshed persisted selection eligibility, rejected new
  inactive catalog references, retained already-materialized inactive references,
  enforced repeatable-source distinctness, and rejected removed Wizard bookkeeping.
