1. **`src/party/storage/contracts.ts` is a LEAF.** It may import the existing
   `Brand` helper from `src/domain/ids.ts` (itself a leaf, type-only) and
   nothing else from the app. No import of `src/db`, `src/worker`,
   `src/backup`, `src/sharing`, `src/ui`. Reuse `Brand` — do not invent a
   second branding mechanism for `RepositoryRevision` / `RepositoryPath` /
   `PartyPublicationId`.
2. **Transcribe section 4's types, do not paraphrase them.** All seven
   `StorageResult` arms with their exact payload fields (including
   `not-found.at`, `conflict.expected|actual` nullable, `unauthorized`'s five
   `CredentialState` members, `rate-limited.retryAt`, `network-failed`'s
   three-valued `writeState`, `too-large`'s nullable observed/limit). Every
   consumer switches exhaustively with no `default:` arm; untrusted JSON
   starts as `unknown`.
3. **`WriteCondition` must make an unconditional update unrepresentable** —
   `create-only` or `replace(expected)`, no third arm, no optional expected.
4. **The forge dispatch must be total AND must not become a three-lane merge
   conflict.** P1-GH, P1-GL and P1-CB run as three parallel lanes; if all
   three must edit one `switch`, they collide by construction. Ship
   `src/party/storage/create-storage.ts` taking a closed
   `Readonly<Record<Forge, (config: RepositoryConfig) => PartyStorage>>`, so a
   missing forge is a compile error and each adapter lane creates only its own
   module. That satisfies section 4's "exhaustive typed dispatch, no
   `default:` arm" as well as the doc's illustrative `switch` does; if you
   keep the `switch` instead, say why in the report.
5. **`Forge` stays exactly `'github' | 'gitlab' | 'codeberg'`.** Your
   fixture-backed test adapter (below) implements `PartyStorage` and is
   constructed directly by tests. It is NEVER a fourth `Forge` member and
   never appears in the dispatch record — adding it would turn
   PARTY-FORGE-EXHAUSTIVE into a lie.
6. **`CredentialLease` is defined here, implemented in P2.** Section 12 says
   "P2 depends only on P0's credential-facing contract". The lease applies
   authorization headers to an outgoing request; it exposes no string-valued
   token property, no `toJSON`, and is not structured-cloneable across a
   Worker boundary. Include the anonymous arm (section 6: public reads without
   manufacturing an empty token). P0 reads and writes NO `sessionStorage`, and

7. **The harness cannot reach the network, and cannot be made to.** Fixtures
